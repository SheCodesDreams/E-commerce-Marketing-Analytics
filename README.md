# E-Commerce Marketing Analytics & Customer Segmentation

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on customer acquisition, marketing campaign performance, customer behavior, and customer segmentation for an e-commerce business.

The project combines **Python, Pandas, SQL, SQLite, and Power BI** to transform raw e-commerce data into actionable business insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The goal of this project is to understand:

- Which acquisition channels perform best?
- Which marketing campaigns are most efficient?
- How does revenue change over time?
- Which customer segments generate the most value?
- Which customers are at risk of disengagement?
- Where should marketing budget be prioritized?

The project covers the complete analytics workflow from data cleaning and validation to business analysis and dashboard development.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Evaluate customer acquisition performance across marketing channels
- Measure campaign performance using revenue, CAC, and ROAS
- Analyze customer purchasing and repeat-purchase behavior
- Identify high-value and at-risk customer segments
- Analyze revenue and acquisition trends over time
- Develop actionable marketing and retention recommendations
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for business reporting

---

## 📊 Dataset

The project uses five primary datasets:

| Dataset | Records | Description |
|---|---:|---|
| Customers | 200,000 | Customer demographics, engagement, loyalty and account information |
| Sessions | 2,000,000 | Website sessions, traffic sources, devices, campaign interactions and browsing behavior |
| Transactions | 500,000 | Orders, order value, item counts, payment methods and shipping information |
| Campaigns | 200 | Marketing campaign type, budget, target region and campaign dates |
| Geographic Data | 100 | Regional income, urbanization and internet penetration information |

The raw datasets contain approximately **2.7 million records** in total.

> The raw datasets are not included in this repository because of their size. Processed analytical datasets used in the project are included in the `outputs/` directory.
